**Junteng Liu** is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, where he is supervised by Professor Junxian He. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with research interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Academic Background
